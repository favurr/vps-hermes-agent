## Lead Angle

Sahar's Food Lab has a unique rotating-menu hook that drives repeat Instagram traffic, but the schedule and menu are only discoverable by scrolling posts. That makes catering inquiries and new-customer visits harder than they need to be. A simple one-page site with current menu, location/schedule widget, and a catering/event form would turn curious scrollers into repeat customers and event bookings. It also opens the door to a fuller business app for inventory, daily specials, and prepaid orders.
