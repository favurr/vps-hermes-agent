## Lead Angle

The Boonseek already has strong demand and a loyal following, but everything depends on Instagram Stories for schedule and menu discovery. That’s fragile. A one-page site with menu, weekly schedule, and a contact form would make the brand easier to find on Google, easier for event organizers to book, and easier to convert casual scrollers into customers. It also creates a foundation for a fuller business app with inventory, daily specials, and optional prepaid orders.
